## Direction
Deep navy #102B43, brand blue #196FA7, white #FFFFFF and cool pale gray #F3F6F8. Restrained semibold sans-serif typography, readable body copy, spacious sections, quiet borders, small-radius buttons and inputs.
The website logo preserves the circular blue emblem, crossed claw hammers, pitched roofs and window. Text is moved beside the emblem for header legibility.
The full-width hero uses actual supplied crew-photo reference with an intentional navy gradient overlay for text contrast. Project gallery references actual supplied concrete, stair and masonry photos. Use original JPEGs directly during implementation; generated mockup photo representations can differ in fine details.
Desktop content is divided into three readable section images rather than compressed into one long image.
