## Requirements and direction
- Company: Jevvy Construction Inc, construction corporation based in Jamaica, NY.
- Audience: prospective project owners and contractors assessing work and qualifications.
- Primary action: discuss a project. Secondary action: explore work.
- Homepage flow: navigation → hero → qualification summary → introduction → expertise → work gallery → qualifications → contact CTA → contact/social footer.
- Visual direction: editorial construction identity, white surfaces, charcoal portfolio/footer, orange accents, bold grotesk typography, square photos, fine divider lines.
- Temporary text wordmark JEVVY. / CONSTRUCTION INC: no official logo was located in the supplied image folder or visible Instagram profile. Replace with verified company logo when supplied.
- 44 supplied site photos reviewed in contact sheet. Selected original photos are the evidence for proposed concrete/sidewalk, masonry/blockwork and stair categories. Confirm final services wording before publication.
- Photos in generated mockups are AI-rendered representations of supplied references; use original JPEG files directly in final implementation.
- No invented ratings, project totals, years in business, warranties, testimonials or agency-client endorsements.
